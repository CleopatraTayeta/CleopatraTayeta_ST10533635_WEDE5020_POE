Our Projects

Technology in service of conservation, from the field to the lab.

RoboWild runs and supports a range of robotics and IT initiatives designed with our partner reserves and conservation organisations. Each project pairs volunteer technologists with the specific needs of the animals and rangers they serve.

Wildlife Monitoring

Drone and camera-trap networks, combined with lightweight sensor tags, allow partner reserves to track herd movements and detect distressed or injured animals earlier than traditional patrols alone.

  • Deployed across 6 partner reserves
  • Real-time alerts sent to ranger devices
  • Built using low-cost, solar-powered hardware

Anti-Poaching Technology

RoboWild volunteers build and maintain affordable tracking and perimeter-alert systems, developed in direct collaboration with ranger teams to fit the realities of each reserve.

  • GPS-linked alert systems for high-risk zones
  • Custom-built for low connectivity areas
  • Maintained through ongoing volunteer support

Training Workshops

Technology is only useful if teams know how to use and maintain it. RoboWild runs hands-on workshops teaching rangers and animal-care staff the basics of the robotics and IT tools deployed on their reserve.

  • Workshops run quarterly at each partner site
  • Covers equipment use, basic repairs and data reading
  • Delivered by volunteer robotics and IT specialists
